# "Did you know?" trivia backlog

Candidate facts for the planned "Did you know?" popups (feature E2 in `02-features.md`). The bar for adding one is in `CLAUDE.md`. Each entry names the item, the angle (trip or media), and the fact.

When the feature is built, these move into the content build as curated data, and this file goes away.

| Item | Angle | Fact |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 男 / 女 (kanji) | Trip | Restroom doors and public baths are often marked only with 男 (men) and 女 (women), sometimes in plain text with no pictogram. Some trains have women-only cars marked 女性専用車 at rush hour. |
| 入 / 出 (kanji) | Trip | Station signs label entrances 入口 (いりぐち) and exits 出口 (でぐち). Big stations number their exits (for example 東口, the east exit), and meeting friends "at exit 3" is normal. |
| 円 (kanji) | Trip | 円 (えん) is the yen. Prices are written like 500円, and the ¥ sign is just the Western way of writing the same currency. |
| 様 (kanji) | Media | The honorific 〜様 (〜さま) is the very polite form of 〜さん. You see it on letters and shop customers (お客様), and in anime it marks a character as very respected, or as someone who thinks they are. |
| ゆ (kana) | Trip | Public baths (銭湯) and hot springs often hang a curtain with a big ゆ on it. It stands for 湯, hot water, so a ゆ curtain means "bath this way". |
